    What Dadblock protects
    • Network filtering: blocks requests matching enabled rules, including covered ads and trackers.
    • Page protection: blocks covered popup attempts, disables certain invisible click traps, and hides identified ads. Hiding an ad does not itself stop its network activity.
    • Scam audit: checks selected readable page content. It does not inspect every script, download, or linked destination.
    • Dad’s report: lists what this page does in your tab, such as outside sites it contacted, password or card boxes, and permission requests. It is computed on your device and is not a safety verdict.
    • Limits: does not hide your IP address, verify every website, or guarantee that browsing is safe.

    Protection depends on your settings and coverage. Local AI analyzes page text on this device; Cloud/BYOK sends selected text for analysis after redaction. Redaction cannot guarantee that every personal detail is removed.
